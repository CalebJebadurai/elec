# Swing model backtest

Rolling origin: 125 assembly elections 2000–2026 with unchanged boundaries, 17211 seats. Each is forecast by a model fitted only on elections before its year.

| Model | Seat winners right (95% CI) | Largest party right | Mean seat error per party | Brier score per seat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0: same party wins again | 44.8% (41.9%–48.4%) | 53 / 125 | – | 1.104 |
| M1: app engine today (10% anti-incumbency) | 44.4% (42.0%–47.3%) | 57 / 125 | 9.7 | – |
| **Swing model** | **50.6% (48.1%–53.9%)** | **71 / 125** | **8.1** | **0.657** |

- Swing model minus persistence, seat accuracy: 5.7% (95% CI 3.3% to 9.1%).
- 80% seat ranges contained the actual count for **79.9%** of parties (target 80%).
- Mean probability the model gave to the party that actually came first: 49.8%.
